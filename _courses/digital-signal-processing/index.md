Digital Signal Processing (DSP) is a fundamental area of electrical engineering that deals with the processing of signals in digital form. This course covers the theory, algorithms, and practical applications of DSP.

## Course Overview

- Learn the fundamentals of digital signal processing
- Understand Fourier transforms and frequency domain analysis
- Explore filter design techniques
- Implement DSP algorithms in practice

For more details and interactive content, visit the [full course on GitBook](https://zshujon.gitbook.io/digital-signal-processing/).
