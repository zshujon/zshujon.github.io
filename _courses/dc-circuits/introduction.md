## Introduction to DC Circuits

Direct Current (DC) circuits form the foundation of electrical engineering.

### Basic Concepts

#### Voltage (V)
Electric potential difference between two points, measured in Volts (V).

#### Current (I)
Flow of electric charge, measured in Amperes (A).

#### Resistance (R)
Opposition to current flow, measured in Ohms (Ω).

### Ohm's Law

V = I × R

This fundamental relationship governs DC circuit behavior.

### Power

P = V × I = I²R = V²/R

Measured in Watts (W).

### Circuit Elements

- **Voltage Sources**: Batteries, power supplies
- **Current Sources**: Constant current generators
- **Resistors**: Limit current flow
- **Conductors**: Wires connecting components

### Series and Parallel Circuits

**Series**: Same current through all elements
**Parallel**: Same voltage across all elements
