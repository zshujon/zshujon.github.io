## Mechanical Properties of Materials

Mechanical properties determine how materials respond to applied forces.

### Stress and Strain

- **Stress (σ)**: Force per unit area
- **Strain (ε)**: Deformation per unit length

### Stress-Strain Relationship

The relationship is characterized by:
- Elastic region
- Yield point
- Plastic region
- Ultimate strength
- Fracture point

### Young's Modulus

E = σ/ε (in elastic region)

### Common Mechanical Tests

1. **Tensile Test**: Measures strength and ductility
2. **Hardness Test**: Resistance to indentation
3. **Impact Test**: Energy absorption capacity
4. **Fatigue Test**: Cyclic loading performance
