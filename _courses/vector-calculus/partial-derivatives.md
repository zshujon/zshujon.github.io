## Partial Derivatives and Gradients

Extending calculus to functions of multiple variables.

### Partial Derivatives

For f(x, y, z):

∂f/∂x: derivative with respect to x (y, z constant)
∂f/∂y: derivative with respect to y (x, z constant)
∂f/∂z: derivative with respect to z (x, y constant)

### Gradient Vector

∇f = (∂f/∂x)**i** + (∂f/∂y)**j** + (∂f/∂z)**k**

### Properties of Gradient

- Points in direction of maximum increase
- Magnitude is rate of maximum increase
- Perpendicular to level surfaces

### Directional Derivative

D_**u**f = ∇f · **u**

where **u** is a unit vector.

### Applications

- Optimization: Finding maxima/minima
- Level curves and surfaces
- Normal vectors to surfaces
- Temperature gradients
- Potential fields

### Chain Rule

For composite functions:
dz/dt = (∂z/∂x)(dx/dt) + (∂z/∂y)(dy/dt)
