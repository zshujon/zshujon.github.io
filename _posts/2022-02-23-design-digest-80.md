## Design Digest #80

Welcome to this week's design digest! We've curated the best articles, tools, and resources from around the web to keep you inspired and informed.

### Featured Articles

#### The Future of Web Design

The web design landscape is shifting rapidly. Key trends to watch:

- **3D Elements** - WebGL and Three.js making 3D mainstream
- **Micro-interactions** - Small animations that delight users
- **Dark Mode** - Now a standard expectation
- **Glassmorphism** - Frosted glass aesthetic
- **Minimalism 2.0** - Simplified but not boring

#### Accessibility in Modern Design

Creating inclusive experiences is no longer optional:

**Key Principles:**
1. Color contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum)
2. Keyboard navigation support
3. Screen reader compatibility
4. Focus indicators
5. Alternative text for images
