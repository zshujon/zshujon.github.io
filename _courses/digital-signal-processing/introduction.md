## Introduction to Digital Signal Processing

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of digital signal processing.

### Topics Covered

- What is Digital Signal Processing?
- Analog vs Digital Signals
- Applications of DSP
- Course Overview

### Key Concepts

DSP is the processing, analysis, and manipulation of signals in digital form. Unlike analog signal processing, digital signal processing operates on discrete-time signals represented as sequences of numbers.
