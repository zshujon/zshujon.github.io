## Introduction to Material Properties

Understanding material properties is fundamental to engineering design and material selection.

### Classification of Materials

Materials are generally classified into:
- **Metals**: High strength, ductile, good conductors
- **Ceramics**: Hard, brittle, heat resistant
- **Polymers**: Lightweight, flexible, insulators
- **Composites**: Engineered combination of materials

### Fundamental Properties

#### Mechanical Properties
- Strength and hardness
- Elasticity and plasticity
- Toughness and ductility
- Fatigue and creep resistance

#### Physical Properties
- Density
- Thermal conductivity
- Electrical conductivity
- Optical properties

### Material Selection Criteria

Engineers consider:
1. Performance requirements
2. Manufacturing processes
3. Cost and availability
4. Environmental impact
