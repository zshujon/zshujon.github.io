## Electrostatics

Electrostatics deals with stationary electric charges and their fields.

### Coulomb's Law

Force between two point charges:

F = (q₁q₂)/(4πε₀r²)

### Electric Field Intensity

E = F/q = Q/(4πε₀r²) r̂

### Gauss's Law

∮ E·dS = Q/ε₀

### Electric Potential

V = -∫ E·dl

### Capacitance

C = Q/V

### Applications
- Capacitors
- Electrostatic shielding
- Van de Graaff generator
- Electrostatic precipitators
